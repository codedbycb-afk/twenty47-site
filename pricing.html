// Questions owners ask

01

Do I need to be technical?

+

No. You type in plain English. We do the setup with you at your screen and leave written instructions. If you can send a text, you can run this.

02

Can't I just use ChatGPT?

+

You can. You also could do your own taxes. You pay an accountant because they know what to look for and what to skip. Same here: the value is knowing which 3 tools matter for your business and setting them up so they stick.

03

Will this replace my people?

+

We automate tasks, not roles. The goal is your best people doing the work only they can do, and nobody spending Friday afternoon on follow-up texts.

04

What does it cost me outside of Twenty47?

+

A Claude subscription in your name, $20 to $100 a month depending on the plan, plus whatever tools the assessment recommends (usually under $100 a month total). We show the math before you spend anything.

05

What if it doesn't work for my business?

+

The 30 minutes are free. The $999 assessment is refunded if we can't find 5 hours a week. Concierge is month to month. We don't lock you in because the ledger is what keeps you.
