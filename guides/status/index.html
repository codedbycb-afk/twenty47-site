For real estate agents STATUS

6 update texts that answer "where are we?" before anyone asks.

Between contract and closing, your client mostly hears silence. Silence turns into calls, and calls turn into worry. Send these six texts at the six points where clients start wondering, and most of those calls never happen.

The one rule

Send an update on a set day every week, even when nothing changed. "Nothing new, still on track for [date]" is an update. Clients don't need news. They need to know you're watching.

1Under contract

Send the day the contract is signed. Lay out what happens next so nothing surprises them.

Text
We're under contract on [address]. Here's what happens next: 1. Inspection by [date] 2. Appraisal ordered by the lender, usually [X] to [X] days 3. Loan approval by [date] 4. Closing on [date] I'll text you every [day of the week] with where we are, even if nothing has changed. If anything comes up in between, you'll hear from me the same day.

2Inspection is done

Buyer
The inspection is done. I'm going through the report now and I'll call you by [time] to walk through what matters and what doesn't. Most of what's in these reports is normal. We'll focus on the few things worth asking for.
Seller
The buyer's inspection is done. Their agent should send any requests by [date]. When they do, I'll call you right away and we'll decide together. No need to do anything yet.

3Appraisal

Text
Quick update: the appraisal [is scheduled for (date) / came back at (value)]. [We're right where we need to be. / I'll call you in the next hour to talk through options.] Still on track for closing on [date].

4Clear to close

Text
Good news: the lender says we're clear to close. Closing is [date] at [time] at [title company, address]. The lender has to get you the Closing Disclosure at least 3 business days before closing. Read it and send me anything that looks off. I'll send the final checklist [two days before].

5Two days before closing

This is also the moment to warn buyers about wire fraud. Criminals send fake wiring instructions right before closing.

Buyer
Closing is [day] at [time]. Bring your photo ID and [the funds by wire or cashier's check, per title]. Important: wiring instructions will never change by email. Before you wire anything, call [title company] at a number you look up yourself, not one in an email, and confirm the instructions out loud. If anyone emails you new instructions, call me first.
Seller
Closing is [day] at [time]. Bring your photo ID and all keys, garage remotes and codes. Final walkthrough is [day/time]. Leave the house the way the contract says, and you're done.

6The day after closing

Most agents stop at the closing table. This one text is how you become the agent they call next time.

Text
Hope the first night in [the new house / the new place] was a good one. Two things: save my number for anything house related, and I'll check in around the one month mark to see how it's going. Thank you for trusting me with this.
