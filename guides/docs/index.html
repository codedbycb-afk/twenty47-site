For Ohio agents and investors DOCS

The deal folder checklist every document, by stage, in one place.

Contracts in email, receipts in texts, photos in the camera roll. When a lender, title or the Division of Real Estate asks for something, you dig. One folder per deal, set up the same way every time, ends the hunt.

Set up the folder

Make one folder per property, named [Street address], [City]. Inside it, six folders:

Folder structure
01 Listing 02 Offer 03 Under contract 04 Inspection and appraisal 05 Closing 06 After closing

Name every file the same way: date, address, what it is. For example, 2026-11-09 1234 Elm Inspection report.pdf. Files sort themselves by date, and search finds them by street.

When a document arrives by email or text, save it to the folder that day. That one habit does most of the work.

01Listing

  • Written listing agreement. Ohio requires it before you advertise or show the home (ORC 4735.55).
  • Consumer Guide to Agency Relationships, signed by the seller (ORC 4735.56).
  • Anti-Discrimination Disclosure, signed and dated by the seller before you market or show the home. Keep it 3 years after closing (ORC 4735.80).
  • Residential Property Disclosure Form, completed by the owner. Required for most sales of 1 to 4 unit homes, with exemptions such as estate and foreclosure sales (ORC 5302.30).
  • Lead-based paint disclosure and the EPA pamphlet if the home was built before 1978 (40 CFR 745).
  • Point of sale inspection if the city requires one. In Cuyahoga County the rules change city by city. See the POS rule sheet.
  • Listing photos, measurements, and the seller's copies of permits, surveys and warranties.

02Offer

  • Buyer's Consumer Guide to Agency Relationships, signed before the first of these: asking about their finances, showing a home other than at an open house, or discussing an offer (ORC 4735.56).
  • Written buyer agency agreement, before you make an offer for the buyer (ORC 4735.55).
  • Agency Disclosure Statement, signed by the buyer no later than when the offer is prepared, and by the seller before they see the offer (ORC 4735.58).
  • Pre-approval or proof of funds.
  • Every offer and counteroffer, with dates.

03Under contract

  • Signed purchase agreement and every addendum.
  • Lead paint attachment signed by buyer, seller and agents for pre-1978 homes (40 CFR 745.113).
  • Buyer's signed receipt for the Property Disclosure Form. If the buyer got the form after signing, they have 3 business days to rescind, and no later than closing or 30 days after acceptance, whichever comes first (ORC 5302.30).
  • Earnest money receipt and deposit record.
  • A contact sheet: lender, title, inspector, other agent, with phone numbers you've confirmed.

04Inspection and appraisal

  • Home inspection report.
  • Lead inspection or written waiver for pre-1978 homes. Buyers get a 10-day window unless both sides agree otherwise in writing (40 CFR 745.110).
  • Repair requests, the seller's response, and any signed amendment.
  • Appraisal report from the lender.
  • Point of sale report, the repair estimate, and any escrow or buyer assumption form.

05Closing

  • Title commitment, payoff letters, and survey if any.
  • Closing Disclosure. The buyer must get it at least 3 business days before closing (12 CFR 1026.19).
  • Settlement statement for the seller.
  • Wiring instructions confirmed by phone, with the date, time and who you spoke to. See the wire warning guide.
  • Final walkthrough notes.
  • Point of sale certificate or escrow release paperwork.

06After closing

  • Keep the whole folder at least 3 years from the date of the transaction. Ohio requires brokers to keep complete transaction records that long (ORC 4735.18).
  • Recorded deed when it comes back.
  • Commission statement.
  • Add the closing date to your calendar for the anniversary text. See the past client guide.

Flippers and investors, add these

  • Purchase closing statement and loan documents
  • Scope of work, contractor bids and signed contracts
  • Permits and final inspections
  • Every receipt and invoice, saved the day you get it
  • Before, during and after photos, dated
  • Draw requests and lender inspection reports
