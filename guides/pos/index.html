For Cuyahoga County agents POS

The Cuyahoga point of sale rule sheet checked city by city.

Every city in Cuyahoga County sets its own rules for selling a home: whether you need an inspection, what it costs, how much gets held in escrow, and whether the buyer can take over the repairs. Title company lists help, but the ones we checked had wrong fees, wrong addresses and rules that have since changed. So we went to each city's own website and ordinances.

Checked November 9, 2026. Cities change these rules, and some official pages are years old. Call the city's building department before you list. Every row links to the page we read.

How to use it

  1. Check the city at listing, not at contract. Inspections can take weeks to schedule.
  2. Put the deadlines on your calendar: how long the certificate is good for, and how long the seller or buyer has to fix violations.
  3. Plan the escrow early. If repairs won't be done by closing, many cities hold more than the repair estimate.
  4. Save every form in the deal folder. See the deal folder checklist.

Homes on septic

The Cuyahoga County Board of Health point of sale septic evaluation costs $350 on the county's form. It needs 30 days of continuous occupancy and about four weeks of lead time, so order it early. Whether it's required depends on the city.
