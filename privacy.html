Privacy policy

Twenty47 Marketing ("we", "us") is a marketing agency in Cleveland, Ohio. This policy covers our website, twenty47marketing.com, and the automated replies we send on our Instagram account, @twenty47agency.

What we collect

On our website

  • What you send us. Our contact form opens an email to us. We get whatever you write, plus your name and email address.
  • No tracking. We don't use analytics, advertising pixels or tracking cookies on this site.
  • Hosting and fonts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those services receive your IP address and browser details when you visit, as described in their own privacy policies.

On Instagram

When you comment a keyword (such as LIST or TEST) on one of our posts, or send us a keyword by direct message, our app automatically sends you the guide you asked for. To do that, it uses Instagram's official API to read:

  • Comments on our own posts, and the username and comment ID of the person who commented
  • Direct messages sent to our account, and the sender's Instagram user ID and username

We store only the IDs of comments and messages we've already answered, so we don't reply twice, and a log of which username asked for which guide. We don't collect your password, contacts, followers, location or any data from your own account or posts.

How we use it

  • To send you the guide or information you asked for
  • To reply to your messages and questions
  • If you reply with your business details for a 47 Test, to run that test and send you the results

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use it for advertising. We share it only with the services that run our tools (Meta, which operates Instagram, and our hosting provider), or if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep comment and message IDs and the reply log for up to 12 months, then delete them. Emails and messages you send us stay in our inbox until you ask us to delete them, or until we no longer need them to work with you.

Your choices

  • Stop messages. Reply STOP in our Instagram DMs and we won't message you again.
  • Delete your data. See our data deletion instructions, or email us at codedbycb@gmail.com.
  • Ask what we have. Email us, and we'll tell you what we hold about you within 30 days.

Children

Our services are for businesses. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
